Tidal Labs

Leave the phone. Tag your waves. Get the clips.

Film with your iPhone camera, tag waves from your wrist. Import the footage and every ride gets sliced into its own clip, ready to trim, reframe, and share.

How It Works

Simple Setup, Automatic Clips

No camera operator needed. You surf, Tidal Labs handles the rest.

📱

Film with Native Camera

Open the iPhone Camera app, frame the lineup, and set your phone down. Hit record and leave it.

⌚

Tag Waves from Your Wrist

Caught one? Scroll the Digital Crown up on Apple Watch, or press DOWN on your Garmin. Either way you get a precise timestamp for every wave you ride.

📥

Import Your Footage

Back on the sand, open Tidal Labs and import the clip from your camera roll. Watch data syncs automatically.

✂️

Get Your Clips

The app matches timestamps to your footage and trims each wave into its own clip. Open the session to swipe through every ride, then trim and reframe the keepers.

Features

Everything a Surfer Needs

Designed for solo surfers who want their sessions captured without a film crew.

⌚

Watch-Powered Timestamps

Tag a wave with the Digital Crown on Apple Watch or the DOWN button on Garmin. Start and end sessions from your wrist. Each tag logs a precise timestamp used to trim your clip.

✂️

Automatic Clip Trimming

The app matches Watch timestamps to your iPhone footage and trims each ride into its own clip automatically.

📂

Session Library

All your sessions and clips are organized by date and location, each one showing a real frame from the water. Review your progression over time.

🔒

Private by Default

Your footage and session data stay on your device. We never upload your clips to any server.

🎞️

Share Your Best Waves

Export individual clips to your Camera Roll and share anywhere with one tap. Clips export video-only, so files stay small and playback never interrupts your music.

🏄

Solo Surfer Friendly

No buddies, no operators. Just you, your gear, the native Camera app, and a fixed iPhone angle.

📍

Auto Spot Detection

After each session, the app uses your location to automatically name the nearest surf spot via Surfline.

❤️

Workout Tracking

Apple Watch logs your session as an outdoor workout in the Health app with heart rate data. On Garmin, each session records as a surfing activity with one lap per wave, plus GPS track and heart rate, and syncs to Garmin Connect like any other activity.

🧭

Garmin SupportNew

Tag waves from a fenix, Instinct, Forerunner, Venu, or vívoactive. Physical buttons only, because a wet touchscreen taps itself. Wave times push to your iPhone over Bluetooth.

🎬

Full-Session Clip EditorNew

Sessions open straight into a landscape player. Swipe between waves, trim the dead air off either end, and reframe any clip. Cropping is non-destructive, so the original framing is always one tap away.

🎯

Auto-Follow CropBeta

Optional. Uses your session GPS track and on-device person detection to pan and zoom each clip so you stay in frame. Experimental, so it ships off. Turn it on in Settings.

⏱️

Configurable Clip Length

Set how far back each tag reaches, from 30 seconds to 3 minutes. Tune it to your style of surfing.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Tidal Labs ("the App") is developed and operated by Micah Woodring. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

Tidal Labs is designed to process all data locally on your device. Specifically, we may collect or access:

  • Photo Library access — to import video footage you recorded with the native Camera app from your camera roll
  • Apple Watch motion and sensor data — to detect and timestamp wave events during your surf session
  • Location — used after import to identify the nearest surf spot via the Surfline API. If you enable Auto-Follow Crop, the watch also records a GPS track during the session and the phone reads its own position once at import, so the App can work out where you were relative to the camera
  • Bluetooth — used only to talk to a paired Garmin watch and pull the wave times it recorded
  • HealthKit — the Apple Watch logs your session as an outdoor surfing workout with heart rate data, stored in the Health app on your device
  • Local storage — video clips and session data are stored on your device only
  • App usage data — anonymous crash logs and diagnostics may be collected through Apple's standard crash reporting, subject to your system-level privacy settings

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, upload, or store your video footage on any external server
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not share any data with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes
  • Location data leaves your device only as an approximate position sent to the Surfline API to name the nearest surf spot. GPS tracks recorded for Auto-Follow Crop stay on your device
  • We do not run any facial recognition, and we do not identify who appears in your footage

3. Apple Watch Data

Sensor and motion data from your Apple Watch is used exclusively to detect wave timestamps during an active recording session. When Auto-Follow Crop is enabled, the Watch also records a GPS track for the duration of the session. This data is processed on-device and is not transmitted anywhere by the App.

4. Garmin Watches

The App can pair with a compatible Garmin watch running the Tidal Labs Connect IQ app. Pairing and communication use the Garmin Connect IQ SDK, which reaches the watch over Bluetooth and routes the paired-device list through the Garmin Connect Mobile app. Only wave timestamps are pulled from the watch, and they are stored on your device.

Separately from the App, your Garmin watch records the session as a standard surfing activity in a FIT file, including lap times, GPS track, and heart rate. That activity syncs to Garmin Connect through Garmin's own software, under Garmin's privacy policy and your Garmin account settings. Tidal Labs neither controls nor receives that sync.

5. Photo Library

The App requires Photo Library access to import video footage you recorded with the native Camera app. The App does not record video itself. Imported footage is processed on-device and is never uploaded by the App.

6. Video Analysis

Auto-Follow Crop is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, the App analyzes imported footage on your device using Apple's Vision framework to locate a person in frame, combined with the GPS track from your session, so it can pan and zoom each clip. The analysis runs entirely on-device, no frames or detection results leave your phone, and no faces are recognized or identified.

7. Third-Party Services

Tidal Labs does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data broker services. The App does send your approximate GPS location to the Surfline public API solely to identify the nearest surf spot name for your session label. No personal information is transmitted in this request.

If you use a Garmin watch, the App uses Garmin's Connect IQ SDK and the Garmin Connect Mobile app to reach your watch. Your use of Garmin devices and software is governed by Garmin's own terms and privacy policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact
