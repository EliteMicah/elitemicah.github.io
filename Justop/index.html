Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Justop ("the App") is developed and operated by Micah.Tech. This Privacy Policy explains what the App stores, what it does not, and who can see it.

The short version: Justop collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. The App makes no network requests of its own. Everything it stores lives on your iPhone and is deleted with the App.

1. Data We Collect

None. Justop does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no email address, and no device identifier sent anywhere.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App keeps the following in a private App Group container on your iPhone. It never leaves the device, and it is not backed up to any service operated by the developer:

  • Your schedules — the name, days of the week, and start and end times you enter
  • Your app selections — stored as opaque tokens issued by iOS (see section 3)
  • Unlock session state — whether an unlock timer is running and when it started
  • Trial and purchase state — the date you first opened the App, and whether the one-time unlock has been purchased
  • Vacation mode counters — how many of the month's allowance you have used

3. Screen Time and Family Controls

Justop uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) with individual authorization. This is the only mechanism iOS provides for one app to hide another, and it is why the App asks for Screen Time access on first launch.

When you pick apps, categories, or websites to block, iOS hands the App opaque tokens rather than names or bundle identifiers. The App is never told which apps you selected. It cannot read them, log them, or transmit them, because it never has them in a readable form.

Justop does not read your Screen Time usage statistics, pickup counts, notification counts, or any usage history. It does not use Screen Time data for advertising, profiling, marketing, analytics, or any purpose other than applying the blocks you configured on the device where you configured them.

4. Network Activity

The App makes no network requests. Two buttons hand off to destinations outside the App, and neither one sends your data anywhere:

  • "Fix in Settings" opens the iOS Settings app on your device
  • "Redeem a code" opens Apple's redemption page at apps.apple.com, because iOS provides no in-app redemption sheet for one-time purchases

5. Purchases

Justop offers a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and your App Store account. Payment details are processed by Apple and are never seen by, sent to, or stored by the developer. The App checks only whether a verified purchase exists for your Apple Account, and records that result locally as a yes or no. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, or data-broker SDKs. If you have opted in to share diagnostics with Apple in your iOS settings, Apple may collect standard crash reports under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive personally identifying information from those reports.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All App data lives in the App's own container on your device. Deleting Justop deletes it. Because nothing is transmitted or stored off-device, there is no server-side copy to request, export, or erase.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and no personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

9. This Website

This page uses GoatCounter for aggregate visit counts. It sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses, and does not track visitors across sites. It is entirely separate from the App, which contacts nothing.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision, and continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of it.

Last updated: September 2026

1. What the App Does

Justop applies iOS Screen Time restrictions to the apps, categories, and websites you select, during the schedules you set. Once a schedule is running, the App's own unlock path requires holding a four-hour session with Justop in the foreground.

2. Trial and Purchase

Justop is free to use for two days from first launch. After that, a one-time purchase of $4.99 (or the equivalent in your local currency) unlocks the App permanently. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

When the trial ends without a purchase, blocking stops: existing blocks are lifted, no new blocks are applied, and creating schedules, editing schedules, and starting unlock sessions are unavailable until the App is purchased. Your schedules are kept and resume working the moment you purchase.

The trial period is tracked on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the App restarts it.

3. Purchases and Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple through your App Store account and are subject to Apple's terms. Refunds are handled solely by Apple, at Apple's discretion, through reportaproblem.apple.com. The developer cannot issue refunds directly. Restoring a previous purchase is available from the paywall and from Settings.

4. What the App Cannot Do

iOS always leaves you a way out, and no third-party app can close it. You can turn off Justop's Screen Time access in Settings, or delete the App, and every block lifts immediately. Justop is a commitment device, not a security product. Do not rely on it where a failure to block would cause you harm or loss.

5. Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for the apps and schedules you choose. Do not block apps you may need in an emergency. Emergency calling, Messages, and other core system functions are outside what Screen Time shields, but you should still configure the App with that in mind.

Support

Justop for iPhone • iOS 16 and later

Email micahs.tech@proton.me with any question, bug, or request. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Nothing is being blocked

Check three things: Screen Time access is granted (Settings inside Justop shows "granted"), the schedule is enabled, and the current time falls inside the schedule's window. Blocks apply at the start of a window, so a schedule created mid-window may not shield until Justop is brought to the foreground.

The blocks disappeared

Either the trial ended without a purchase, vacation mode is running, or Screen Time access was turned off in iOS Settings. The Home screen shows a banner for the last of these.

I bought it on another device

Tap "Restore purchase" on the paywall, or Settings → Purchase. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account.

I want the blocks to be harder to escape

iOS lets any user revoke a third-party app's Screen Time access or delete the app. To close that door, ask someone you trust to turn on Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions, set iTunes & App Store Purchases → Deleting Apps to "Don't Allow", and protect it with a Screen Time passcode you do not know.
